# Small MLP and effect-modifier signal ablations

These two additive binary experiments reuse the original
`SyntheticEffectModifier` generator, outcome-contrast evaluator, multiround
FedAvg implementation, iterative dsLassoCov-style logistic LASSO and original
Diag/KFAC FedFisher CPU recipes. Existing recipes and result namespaces remain
separate. The new factory setting is `SyntheticMLPSmall`, with one ReLU hidden
layer of eight neurons: `201 -> 8 -> 2`, including biases, **1,634 parameters**.
The existing shallow `(64, 32)` and deep `(256, 128, 64, 32)` networks retain
their settings.

| Setting | Effect-modifier strength | MLP architectures | Method configurations |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| `small_mlp` | 2.0 | Small only | 10 |
| `weak_signal_1` | 1.0 | Small, shallow, deep | 31 |
| `weak_signal_05` | 0.5 | Small, shallow, deep | 31 |

Each architecture uses all eight original FedAvg settings: local epochs
`1, 5, 10, 30`, each with validation patience and fixed total budget 30. The
budget-30/epoch-30 recipe still has one aggregation round. Learning rate,
momentum, batches, stopping, selection, refitting and random-seed rules are
copied from the comparison protocol. Each architecture also uses the original
standalone CPU Diag and KFAC recipes, including 30 local epochs, 2,000 server
iterations, compression, validation selection and selected-Fisher RNG isolation.
KFAC retains its existing architecture-dependent compression rule.

Each weak-signal setting includes **one** iterative dsLassoCov configuration;
it has no MLP architecture. Its original five folds, 30 lambda values and
2,000-iteration optimizer limit remain unchanged. A numerical limit is not
convergence and cannot produce accepted completion evidence.

Only `dgp.signal_strength` changes for the weak-signal recipes. The outcome
interaction coefficients for `A*X1` and `A*X2` are respectively plus/minus
`signal_strength / sqrt(2)`. Prognostic main effects and confounding strength
both remain 1.0. Outcomes and outcome-dependent non-IID assignments are
regenerated by the original generator; identical datasets across different
signal strengths are not required. Within a signal setting, all methods share
the prescribed data and intervention plans.

Every setting retains all four factorial variants (`control`,
`site_gaussian_shift`, `local_permutation`, `site_gaussian_local_permutation`),
IID and non-IID splits, and the original data/evaluation sizes. The primary
cross-method endpoint is raw-X probability-contrast intervention recovery with
explicit tie handling; logit-contrast recovery remains available separately.
Training, tuning/refitting, aggregation and evaluation costs remain distinct.

The seed-zero pilot comprises 288 requests and 576 scientific units. Formal
scope is seeds 0..299: 24,000 small-MLP units and 148,800 weak-signal units,
172,800 in total. Formal shards are disjoint deterministic sequences at 8 or
16 shards per setting/method/variant. Run IDs begin with
`model_signal_<setting>_<cell>_<variant>_v1_`; old outputs are never reused.

## Implementation and admission

`protocol.py` composes copies of the existing fixed recipes. `runner.py`
dispatches to the existing scientific functions. `artifacts.py` reuses the
immutable timing store and each method family's existing semantic validator.
`acceptance.py` verifies the complete pilot, paired invariants and actual
central producer evidence. Formal publication and runtime both require the
same-source accepted pilot proof; central resource review is still required.
Formal startup revalidates all 576 pilot units and their producer evidence.
This verification adds filesystem work outside measured method fitting time;
operator profiling must account for that startup cost.

The sole execution entrypoint remains the project ServerScheduler adapter.
New task names are `effect-modifier-model-signal-pilot` and
`effect-modifier-model-signal-shard`. The proposal in
`../server_scheduler/model-signal-ablation-v1/` is disabled. No direct compute
CLI or launcher is provided. Development fixture tests are not pilot evidence.

After canonical-source integration, clean source freeze and operator review,
`scripts/prepare_model_signal_requests.py` can prepare an immutable pilot
review bundle. `scripts/validate_model_signal_pilot.py` validates its completed
scientific outputs. The builder's formal mode requires that acceptance proof.
Creating an outbox bundle does not submit jobs or authorize formal execution.

Source belongs in the repository; durable results belong below
`/data/del6500/FedFisher/effect_modifier_experiment/outputs/`; development test
logs/caches and request review bundles belong below
`/scr/del6500/FedFisher/`. Keep canonical source unchanged while any admitted
cohort relies on it. Record changing validation/integration facts only in
`docs/codex/SCHEDULER_HANDOFF.md` or post-freeze scratch evidence.

## Recovery epoch: explicit budget exhaustion

`recovery_protocol.py`, `recovery.py`, and `recovery_acceptance.py` add a
separate execution contract for the same scientific recipes. The two task
names are `effect-modifier-model-signal-recovery-pilot` and
`effect-modifier-model-signal-recovery-shard`. V2 result namespaces never
overwrite or resume a partial V1 directory. The original V1 tasks continue to
reject scientific noncompletion.

FedAvg validation-patience training retains its **200-round** safety bound,
patience 10, `min_delta=1e-4`, original SGD, local epochs, selection and refit
rules. A 200-round fit that has not satisfied patience returns its selected
training-subset checkpoint without a full-data refit. This is an explicit
`validation_patience_limit_200` outcome, not convergence or a performance
estimate. The recovery task validates the complete finite round history,
patience replay, selected checkpoint, model arrays, site counts, communication,
timing and identities before saving this outcome. No other fitting exception
is eligible for continuation; unknown errors, nonfinite values and invalid
evidence stop the shard.

Successful units keep `started.json`, `model.npz`, `result.json`, and
`done.json`. Budget-exhausted units instead keep `started.json`,
`diagnostic_model.npz`, `noncompletion.json`, and `noncompletion_marker.json`.
They contain no evaluation metrics and never obtain a `done.json` marker.
Each started record binds its closeout and applicable new pilot prerequisite.
A retry may skip either fully validated terminal state while preserving its
actual producing attempt. Partial or corrupted directories remain immutable
and fail closed.

A shard writes its exact seed/split outcome inventory under
`shard_summaries/<job_id>.json`. `execution_complete` means every assigned unit
has a validated terminal outcome; `whole_shard_scientific_success` is false
whenever any unit exhausted its budget. A central zero exit status therefore
does not assert that all models met their scientific stopping rule.

The recovery pilot has 290 requests / 588 units: all 288 original seed-zero
configurations (576 units), plus two Gaussian-arm diagnostics covering seeds
9, 25 and 41 under both splits. All 576 seed-zero successes and the six known
nonzero-seed successes must remain successful and numerically identical to
their archived counterparts, except measured timings and peak memory. Both
seed-25/non-IID failures must reproduce the verified cap. Only the four
previously unattempted seed-41 units may newly finish successfully or exhaust
their unchanged budget. Diagnostics must proceed beyond the capped unit.

An immutable frozen-source closeout explicitly binds retained V1 successes to
their original manifests, source and producers. The recovery protocol fixes
this closeout's path and digest. Known successful units in failed or
unknown-exit shards remain audit/parity material and are not retained as
centrally successful formal shards. New formal coverage is the exact
complement of the retained complete shards, under new IDs and 16-way
deterministic sequences. The final composite contract covers all 172,800
planned units exactly once.

Reporting retains the **300-seed denominator**, counts successful and
budget-exhausted units separately, and exposes all missing/error states.
Performance estimates from successful units are conditional on successful
completion. Paired comparisons require actual matching successful results
and report their coverage. Diagnostic checkpoints never fill missing
performance values. A cohort may be operationally complete while its
`whole_cohort_scientific_success` remains false.

After a clean source freeze, `scripts/prepare_model_signal_recovery_requests.py`
publishes only the new pilot bundle. The saved-evidence validator
`scripts/validate_model_signal_recovery.py` must accept the actual centrally
executed pilot before the builder or runtime admits formal recovery. The
disabled proposal is under `../server_scheduler/model-signal-recovery-v2/`.
Same-source evidence checks include startup I/O beyond fitting time; the
operator must account for that cost. Outbox files remain delivery artifacts,
and only ServerScheduler admits and executes them.
